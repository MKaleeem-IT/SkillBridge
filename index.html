HOW IT WORKS

From Skills to Real Opportunities

SkillBridge makes it simple for students to find real projects and for businesses to find skilled talent.

01

Create Your Profile

Students and businesses create profiles with their skills, services, experience and requirements.

02

Find Opportunities

Students explore available projects while businesses post projects with budgets and deadlines.

03

Connect & Collaborate

Businesses review applications and portfolios, then select the right student for their project.

04

Complete & Grow

Projects are completed, feedback is shared and students build valuable experience and portfolios.
