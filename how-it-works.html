HOW IT WORKS

From Skills to Real Opportunities

SkillBridge makes it simple for students and businesses to connect, collaborate and complete projects.

FOR STUDENTS

Start Building Your Experience

Follow a simple process to find projects, work with businesses and grow your portfolio.

01

Create Your Profile

Add your skills, experience, portfolio, availability and service preferences.

02

Find Projects

Browse projects posted by businesses and discover opportunities matching your skills.

03

Apply for Projects

Submit your proposal and show businesses why you are suitable for their project.

04

Complete & Grow

Complete projects, receive feedback, build your portfolio and gain experience.

FOR BUSINESSES

Find the Right Student Talent

Businesses can post projects, review applications and connect with suitable student talent.

01

Create Business Profile

Create your business profile and provide relevant information about your organization.

02

Post a Project

Describe your project, required skills, budget and expected deadline.

03

Review Talent

Review student profiles, portfolios and applications before making your selection.

04

Collaborate & Review

Work with the selected student, track the project and provide feedback.
